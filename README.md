# Aura — Financial Model & Investment Memo (FinOrbits Season 1)

**Built during FinOrbits Season 1 Investment Banking Simulation Championship — National Rank 10**

---

## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of FinOrbits Season 1, a national-level investment banking simulation competition. The deliverable included a full financial model, investment memo, and pitch presentation for Aura — evaluated by a panel on IB-standard criteria including valuation rigour, deal structuring, and presentation quality.

Achieved **National Rank 10** out of all participating teams across India.

---

## What's Inside

### 1. Financial Model
- 3-statement integrated model (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow)
- DCF valuation with WACC computation
- Scenario analysis: base, bull, and bear cases
- Key operating and financial assumptions documented

### 2. Investment Memo
- Executive summary with investment thesis
- Business overview and market opportunity
- Financial analysis and valuation summary
- Key risks and mitigants

### 3. Pitch Presentation
- Structured in IB pitch deck format
- Covers deal rationale, valuation, and recommendation
